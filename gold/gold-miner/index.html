How to Play

Space / Click — Launch the claw when it points at a treasure.

T — Use TNT to destroy a Rock or nearby Worms.

L — Activate Lucky Leaf to turn the next Rock into a Diamond.

Escape — Pause the game.

Aim for Gold, Diamonds and the Golden Treasure. Avoid Worms (no reward, wastes time) and Rocks (slow to pull).

Reach the round's gold target to pass. Earn 1–3 stars based on your score.

Spend gold in the shop on TNT, Shields, Lucky Leaves and Claw Speed upgrades.
