🐍 Snake

Head leads. Followers follow.

Difficulty Normal
Snake Length 1
Score 0

Instructions

Move with Arrow Keys or WASD. Eat food to grow followers. Grab the golden big food for bonus growth. Avoid the bombs — they shrink your trail.

  • Regular food: +1 score, +1 follower
  • Big food: +3 score, +2 followers
  • Bomb: removes followers

Normal: self-hits shed your tail. Hell: one touch ends the run.

Press Space or P to pause/resume.

Fill the board to win.
